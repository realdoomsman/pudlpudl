## Scene 2 — Rivers = Pools (0:05–0:14)

**On-screen action:** Camera glides low over the world. Several rivers wind through it, glowing with flow. One river visibly widens as trade particles rush through it; a thin trickle beside it stays narrow, for contrast.

**VO:**
> "This is the market from above. Every river is a live pool. The wider it runs, the more volume flows through it."

**Caption:** Rivers = live pools · Wider = more volume

## Scene 3 — Cast a Net (0:14–0:24)

**On-screen action:** Cursor hovers a river. One click. A net drops across the current and the river lights up **acid yellow**. Trade particles stream through the net; a small fee counter ticks up at the edge of frame while a price marker holds steady inside its range.

**VO:**
> "Cast a net — that's you providing liquidity. Now every trade that flows past pays you a real fee. Pumps, dumps, doesn't matter. Volume is volume."

**Caption:** Cast a net = provide liquidity · Collect real fees on volume *(counter illustrative)*

## Scene 4 — Confluences (0:24–0:31)

**On-screen action:** Pull back. Two, then three acid-yellow rivers flow together at a confluence and braid into one glowing channel. Multiple small nets sit across the merged flow.

**VO:**
> "Where rivers meet, you can mix them — spreading your nets across pools so you're never tied to one coin."

**Caption:** Confluences = mix rivers · Spread your risk

## Scene 5 — Fees Flow Downhill (0:31–0:40)

**On-screen action:** Zoom out to the whole basin. A looping flywheel diagram overlays the water: yields → liquidity → volume → boost & burn → back to yields. Flow cascades downhill into a bright **flagship river**. Cut to clean end frame: **PUDL wordmark** + tagline.

**VO:**
> "Fees flow downhill: real yields pull in liquidity, liquidity pulls in volume, and the flow buys back and burns $PUDL. Cast a net. Collect the fees."

**Caption:** Fees flow downhill · Cast a net. Collect the fees.
